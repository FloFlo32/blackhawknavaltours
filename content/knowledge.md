# Blackhawk Boat Tours knowledge base

Source: https://blackhawknavaltours.com

## About
Blackhawk Boat Tours offers thrilling Chicago River and Lake Michigan adventures aboard
military-grade vessels. We're not just a boat tour, we're an adrenaline-fueled journey
through the heart of Chicago, piloted by Coast Guard licensed captains aboard US Navy-style
rigid-hull inflatable boats (RHIBs). Small, personal experiences for families and groups who
want more than just a view: up-close skyline views, engaging stories about Chicago's
landmarks, and the thrill of high-speed adventure on the water.

## Tours & pricing
- **Adventure Lake and River Tour** (featured): starting at $85/person. High-speed runs on
  the open waters of Lake Michigan aboard our Navy-grade vessels.
- **Lake Adventure Tour**: starting at $60/person. Iconic architecture meets high-speed
  thrills through downtown Chicago.
- **Private Charters**: exclusive experiences for groups and special celebrations, priced
  per group.
- **Custom Adventures**: tell us what you want and we'll build the perfect tour for you.

Tour highlights include Willis Tower, The Merchandise Mart, Centennial Fountain and Arc,
Chicago Harbor Light, and Soldier Field.

## Our vessels
Our fleet consists exclusively of authentic military-spec Rigid-Hull Inflatable Boats
(RHIBs), the same vessels trusted by military forces worldwide, originally designed for
Navy SEAL insertions and Coast Guard rescue missions.
- Capacity: 6 passengers per boat (private groups capped at 14 passengers total)
- Hull: deep-V fiberglass with a multi-chamber inflatable collar
- Engine: Cummins diesel, reaching speeds up to 40 MPH on open water
- Safety gear: USCG-approved life jackets
- Bravo-22 traces its origins to the USS Laboon (DDG 58), stationed at Norfolk, VA.
- Hotel-26 traces its origins to the USS Leyte Gulf (CG 55), stationed at Norfolk, VA, now decommissioned.

## Safety
Coast Guard licensed captains, emergency protocols in place, premium life jackets provided.
Our operations exceed all Coast Guard requirements. 100% safety record, 10,000+ happy riders,
5-star average rating.

## Our story
Blackhawk Boat Tours was founded by Chicago locals who felt the city's incredible waterways
deserved a better way to experience them than the slow, crowded, predictable tours already
out there. Captain Ken spent 20 years in the U.S. Coast Guard as a Federal Boarding Officer
and Search and Rescue specialist, served with the Harry S. Truman battle group in 2003
supporting Operation Enduring Freedom and Iraqi Freedom, and now teaches Naval Science at
William Howard Taft High School in Chicago. He started guiding boat tours on the Hudson and
East Rivers in New York in 2014, moved to Chicago in 2017, and in 2022 bought an ex-Navy RHIB
to start Blackhawk Boat Tours. Our mission: safe, intimate, unforgettable boating experiences
led by highly trained, Coast Guard-licensed captains, in a relaxed, family-friendly,
uncrowded setting.
